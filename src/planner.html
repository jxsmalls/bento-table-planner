Before you cut anything
  • Score face-down, cut face-up. Run the score pass with the show face against the bed so the crushed side ends up inside the finished riser.
  • Thickness is already accounted for. Panel and wall heights are reduced so each folded riser's exterior matches its grid footprint exactly and its top lands on the stated elevation.
  • A 1.5 mm panel spanning over ~10 in will visibly sag under real weight. Add the crossed ribs, or laminate a second panel underneath, before trusting it with hardware.
  • Level 0 tiles cut no riser — they're the flat placemat, and only appear in the plan.
